# Glossary — UV Laser Annealing

**a/c interface** — amorphous/crystalline boundary left by implantation; must be fully molten for defect-free LPER (Ch 2, 3, 5)
**Absorption (penetration) depth δ** — 1/α, depth where intensity drops to 1/e; ∝ λ/4πk (Ch 1)
**Allan deviation** — frequency-stability metric for NEMS resonators (Ch 5)
**Assisted heating** — chuck pre-heat (≤450 °C on LT-3100) that extends activation depth (Ch 1, 6)
**Bamboo structure** — interconnect grains spanning the full line width (Ch 4)
**Beer–Lambert law** — I(z) = I0·e^(−αz) (Ch 1)
**BTI** — bias temperature instability from gate stack traps (Ch 2)
**Box-like profile** — abrupt dopant profile bounded by melt depth (Ch 2)
**CoolCube™** — CEA-Leti sequential 3D integration (Ch 5)
**Crystalline volume fraction fc** — Ic/(Ic+Ia) from Raman deconvolution (Ch 7)
**Deactivation** — loss of metastable active dopants in later thermal steps (Ch 3)
**DHEM** — differential Hall effect metrology for active profiles (Ch 3)
**DIBL** — drain-induced barrier lowering, short-channel metric (Ch 2)
**DLTS** — deep-level transient spectroscopy (Ch 2, 3)
**Dwell time** — effective high-T duration; ns vs µs selects mechanism (Ch 1, 4)
**ED (energy density / fluence)** — J/cm² per pulse; main regime knob (Ch 1)
**EOR defects** — end-of-range implant damage below the a/c interface (Ch 3, 5)
**Explosive Melting / Crystallization (EM)** — thin liquid layer travelling through amorphous material → poly (Ch 3, 2)
**F1 / F2 thresholds** — melt onset / ablation onset fluences (Ch 7)
**Field emission** — tunnelling-dominated contact transport at very high doping (Ch 3)
**LPC** — liquid phase crystallization of a-Si into poly-Si (Ch 4)
**LPER** — liquid phase epitaxial regrowth from a crystalline seed (Ch 3)
**LT-3100** — SCREEN/LASSE step-and-repeat UV LTA tool, 160 ns pulse (Ch 1)
**Melting threshold** — ED at first surface melt; lower for implanted/amorphous material (Ch 2)
**Phase segregation** — Si/C (or Ga/N) separation in compound melts (Ch 6, 8)
**Photothermal vs photochemical** — heat-mediated vs direct bond-breaking processing (Ch 1)
**PUND** — Positive-Up-Negative-Down ferroelectric measurement (Ch 4)
**ρc** — specific contact resistivity, target <1–2e-9 Ω·cm² (Ch 3)
**Secondary Melting (SM)** — surface-initiated melt extending downward (Ch 3)
**Segregation coefficient k** — CS/CL at the l/s interface; k<1 → surface pile-up; k→1 as V rises (Ch 3)
**Semi-damascene (subtractive)** — pattern metal after deposition and anneal (Ch 4)
**SLG** — super lateral growth regime near complete melt (Ch 5)
**Solidification velocity V** — melt-front speed (m/s); master LPER variable (Ch 3)
**SPC** — solid phase crystallization (Ch 4)
**SPER** — solid phase epitaxial regrowth without melt (Ch 3)
**SRA** — spreading resistance analysis (Ch 6)
**Thermal budget** — temperature × time load on a structure; 3D limit ~500 °C for a few hours (Ch 1, 5)
**TRR** — in-situ time-resolved reflectivity for melt detection (Ch 3, 5)
**Wrinkles** — periodic undulation of a capped liquid layer (Ch 4)
