# Executive Summary

## Problem Statement
High-frequency Rs monitor usage increases monitoring cost and consumes tool capacity.

## Objective
Develop a two-tier monitoring strategy to lower cost while maintaining excursion protection.

## Method
- Early warning haze signal monitoring
- Reduced-frequency electrical verification
- Cost-risk optimization model
- Sensitivity analysis

## Results
- 75% monitor frequency reduction
- ~$200K annual savings/toolset
- 15% projected scrap risk reduction

## Technical Skills
- SPC trend analysis
- DOE sensitivity study
- Cost optimization model
- Risk simulation

## Engineering Relevance
Applicable to the preventive maintenance strategy for RTA module fleets.
